# ADD2CAM Human Evidence

## GOAL ID

`ADD2CAM-60`

## ROLE

Govern protocol approval, consented participant use, limitations-first analysis, and the actual promotion/rejection decision.

## PREREQUISITE COMMIT

The exact clean `READY_FOR_HUMAN_PILOT` integration commit, assigned only after Goal 50 acceptance.

## BRANCH / WORKTREE

No autonomous worker branch or worktree. Future execution requires an approved human-study session and separately authorized repository update.

## DEPENDENCIES

`ADD2CAM-50` complete; approved protocol; real participants; required consent and evidence handling.

## OUTCOME

Collect genuine lived-use evidence under the approved protocol and make a limitations-first promote, revise, reject, or stop decision without converting synthetic proof into human evidence.

## PROOF OF DONE

Protocol approval, consent, participant/context sufficiency, withdrawals, invalid sessions, receipts, analysis, limitations, and decision are documented honestly; Gates 9-10 pass only on real evidence.

## OWNED FILES

Future approved versions of `docs/pilots/meaning-preview-v1-protocol.md`, `docs/pilots/meaning-preview-v1-report.md`, and integrator updates to `DECISIONS.md`, `PROGRESS.md`, and `TASK_QUEUE.md`.

## PROTECTED FILES

Participant raw data beyond approved retention; secrets/PHI; donor repos; frozen software evidence; all unrelated repository files.

## SAFETY / PROVENANCE

Consent, withdrawal, minimization, confidentiality, and accurate evidence labels are mandatory. Agents, developers, synthetic fixtures, and model judges are not participants.

## AUTONOMOUS DECISION POLICY

None. An autonomous agent may prepare status or validate structure but may not approve the protocol, consent for a person, fabricate observation, or decide promotion without the required human evidence.

## CONSTRAINTS

Human-in-the-loop by definition. Follow the approved protocol and applicable legal/compliance requirements. Preserve failures and withdrawals.

## ITERATION

Approve protocol, recruit/consent, run bounded sessions, validate evidence sufficiency, analyze, review limitations, and record the decision. Each stage requires its own authority.

## HANDOFF

The Goal 50 handoff must identify the exact build, commit, limitations, draft protocol, and minimum human action needed. Future human work writes the pilot report.

## RETRY / RECOVERY

Invalid sessions remain invalid. Repeat only under protocol rules and fresh consent. Never backfill missing observations or silently exclude adverse evidence.

## STOP

Stop without approved protocol/participants/consent, on withdrawal, sensitive-data risk, legal uncertainty, material protocol deviation, or insufficient evidence.

## COMPLETE

Complete only after authentic human evidence satisfies the controlling goal and an authorized limitations-first decision is recorded. It is intentionally pending during autonomous software completion.
